Privacy and respect

Stays on your device

Settings are stored locally in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no remote code. The only network call PlayPilot makes for itself is the licence check with Polar, and only if you enter a key.

Respects creators

PlayPilot does not circumvent DRM or encryption: encrypted streams are refused and YouTube downloads are blocked. Only download what you have the right to keep.
