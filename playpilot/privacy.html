PlayPilot by Pinchavelho – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Version 1.2

Controller: Jose Pinhao, trading as "Pinchavelho", Estrada V Centenario, Bloco B 3CE, 9360-500 Ponta do Sol, Portugal. Email: opinchavelho@gmail.com.

1. What the extension does NOT do

PlayPilot does not collect, sell or share your browsing history, page content or media you watch. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no remote code.

2. Data stored on your device/browser

  • Settings (on-screen indicator, Shift+mouse-wheel, list of sites where PlayPilot is off) and shortcuts, and – only if you enable it – per-site settings (website hostname + speed/audio settings): chrome.storage.local, on your device. You can delete them in Options or by uninstalling.
  • List of detected streams per tab: chrome.storage.session (memory only, cleared when the tab closes).
  • Installation date (to run the 7-day trial locally) and, only if you enter one, your Polar licence key, its activation ID/label and the last check result: chrome.storage.local, on your device (not synced). "Remove key" in Options deletes the key.

This storage is strictly necessary for the features you request (ePrivacy Directive art. 5(3)).

3. Purchases, licence keys and trial

The 7-day trial (all Pro features, including downloads) runs locally (installation date on your device); no sign-up and no data is sent.

Purchases are made on the checkout of Polar (Polar Software Inc., polar.sh), which acts as merchant of record: Polar is the seller of record, processes the payment (through its payment providers such as Stripe), charges and remits VAT/sales tax and issues the invoice/receipt. Polar collects your email address, billing country/address and payment details under its own privacy policy (https://polar.sh/legal/privacy) and sends you the licence key. We receive from Polar your email address, name if given, country, order date, amount and licence-key status – we never see your card details.

  • What the extension sends to Polar: only when you enter a key, the key, our organisation ID and a random activation label (e.g. "PlayPilot 3fa9c21b") to https://api.polar.sh, to activate it and re-check it about once a day (an offline grace period applies). No browsing data, URLs or media information are sent.
  • Like any internet request, these licence checks reveal your IP address and browser user agent to Polar, which processes them under its own privacy policy. The extension sends the requests without cookies.
  • Purposes and legal bases: providing the licence and support (contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)); accounting and legal obligations (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Recipients: Polar Software Inc. (merchant of record; USA – transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses, see Polar's policy) and its payment providers; our accountant and the tax authorities where required.
  • Refund requests: if you email us for a refund we use your email address and order record only to process it with Polar (contract / legal obligation).
  • Retention: licence data while your licence is active plus 2 years; accounting records as required by law (Portugal: 10 years).

4. Downloads

Media is fetched directly from the website to your computer only when you click Download. Nothing passes through our servers (we have none).

5. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR arts. 15–21): write to opinchavelho@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt) or your local authority.

6. Children

PlayPilot is not directed to children under 13; purchases require being of legal age or parental consent.

7. California and other US residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar US state laws: in the last 12 months we collected, only from buyers, identifiers (email address, name if given) and commercial information (order date, amount, country, licence status), received from Polar, for the business purposes of providing the licence, support and complying with law. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

We do not sell or share personal information (including for cross-context behavioural advertising), and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of consumers under 16.

You may ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing opinchavelho@gmail.com. We will verify the request by matching the email used for the purchase. An authorised agent may act for you with your signed permission. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
