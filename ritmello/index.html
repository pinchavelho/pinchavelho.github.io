What it does

Your speed

From 0.1x to 16x, in steps of 0.1 or with one-click presets. The speed you pick stays as you move through the video.

Less waiting

Skip 10 seconds with a key. With Pro, Ritmello speeds through the silences in lessons and talks, and tells you how much time you saved.

Study tools

With Pro: repeat a part with an A-B loop, bookmark moments, go frame by frame and dim the page with focus mode.

Clearer sound

With Pro: volume up to 600% with a limiter, a 10-band equalizer, Clear voice and Night modes, mono and balance.

(i) What Ritmello can’t do

They’ll always win.

Some things we wanted to build are not possible, or not allowed. Here they are, before you buy.

  1. We thought of: Saving videos to your computer.

    Why not: We left it out on purpose, out of respect for creators. Sales platforms also do not accept this kind of product.

    What it does instead: It only changes how the video already playing is played.

  2. We thought of: Improving the sound of copy-protected videos, or of videos from another site that does not allow it.

    Why not: These videos do not let other apps read their sound. Getting around that would mean breaking the protection.

    What it does instead: Speed still works. Sound effects and skip silences turn off by themselves and Ritmello tells you.

  3. We thought of: Skipping the ads on video sites.

    Why not: It goes against those sites’ rules and against the creators who live from those ads.

    What it does instead: Nothing: ads stay as the site shows them.

  4. We thought of: Working on its own on every site from the moment you install it.

    Why not: That would need permission to read and change every site you visit. We prefer to ask you first.

    What it does instead: It works after one click on the icon. With Pro you choose the sites where it turns on by itself.

  5. We thought of: Working on Chrome’s own pages and on the Chrome Web Store.

    Why not: Chrome does not let any extension into those pages.

    What it does instead: It works on every other web page.

  6. We thought of: Syncing bookmarks and time saved between your computers.

    Why not: We would have to send your data to a server. We promised not to.

    What it does instead: They stay only on this computer, and you can delete them in Options.

Found another case? Write to opinchavelho@gmail.com. We review this list with every version.

Privacy and respect

Stays on your computer

Settings, bookmarks and time saved are stored only in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no remote code. The only network request Ritmello makes is the licence check with Polar, and only if you enter a key.

Respects creators

Ritmello only changes how media that is already playing is played. It does not save or record media, does not get around copy protection (DRM) and does not skip ads.

Questions

Why do I have to click the icon?

So Ritmello can only see the pages you choose. Click the icon or press Alt+Shift+R on a page with video, and it is ready. With Pro you can choose sites, such as your course platform, where it turns on by itself.

Does it work on every video?

It works with the standard video and audio of web pages. On copy-protected videos the speed still works, but sound effects and skip silences turn off by themselves. Chrome’s own pages and the Chrome Web Store are closed to every extension.

What happens when the trial ends?

The Pro features lock and Ritmello goes back to the free version. Your bookmarks stay on your computer. Nothing is charged: there is no card to charge.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes, on your own browsers. The licence is personal: it is for you, not for sharing or reselling.

Do I need an account?

No. Polar emails you a licence key after payment; you paste it into Ritmello’s Options.
