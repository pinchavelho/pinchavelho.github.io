What it does

Rejects cookie banners

Presses “Reject all”, or unticks every optional purpose and saves, on the main consent platforms and on many sites with their own banner.

Sends privacy signals

Global Privacy Control tells every site you do not allow your data to be sold or shared.

You stay in charge

Click the icon to see what it did on the page. Pause it, or turn it off on a site that stops working. Counts stay on your computer.

More with Pro

Blocks known trackers in your own browser, cleans tracking codes from links, closes newsletter pop-ups and more.

What it won’t do

Paywalls and “accept or pay”

PinchaHide never hides or gets around paywalls, “accept or pay” pages, sign-in walls, prices or age checks. Those are the site’s choice.

Its limits

It can’t make a site respect your refusal, and it can’t refuse every banner on every site. The full list, and why, is in the app before you buy.

Questions

Does it accept cookies when it can’t refuse?

No. PinchaHide never presses Accept. If a banner offers no way to refuse, it hides the banner without answering it.

A site stopped working. What do I do?

Click the PinchaHide icon and turn protection off for that site. You can also pause PinchaHide for an hour or until you resume it.

What happens when the trial ends?

The Pro features stop and PinchaHide goes back to the free version, which keeps refusing cookie banners. Your Pro settings stay saved. Nothing is charged: there is no card to charge.

Do I need an account?

No. After payment your licence key is in the Polar customer portal: open it from the link in Polar’s confirmation email or sign in with the email you used to buy; you paste it into PinchaHide’s Options.

Does a PinchaPlay key work in PinchaHide?

No. Each app has its own Pro and its own key.
