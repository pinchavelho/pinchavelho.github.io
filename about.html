About

Tools you can trust

Pinchavelho is a Portuguese word for a small mechanism that makes a device work. That is what we build: small, simple, useful tools.

We started from a real concern: many free extensions and apps collect data, show ads or hide malicious code. So we build our own tools, ones people can trust.

We are an independent, one-person studio based in Portugal. Our software is proprietary (closed source); this website and our policies are public.

Our principles

Privacy first

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. No tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Minimal permissions

We ask only for what a feature needs, and explain every permission in plain words.

Respect for creators

No DRM or encryption circumvention. Downloads from YouTube are blocked.

Freedom with responsibility

You decide how to use your tools, within the law.

Fair price

Core features are free. Extras cost a small one-time price that funds development.

Contact

Questions, bugs, refunds or ideas: opinchavelho@gmail.com. Trader details are in the legal notice.
