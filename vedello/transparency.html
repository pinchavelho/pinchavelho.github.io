Vedello transparency report

In short: Vedello answers cookie banners and blocks trackers inside your browser, with rules bundled in the extension. Your settings, counts and history stay on your computer. The only connection Vedello makes by itself is checking your Pro key with Polar, and only after you enter a key.

Permissions and why

PermissionWhy
storageKeeps your settings, counts, history (Pro), the trial start date and your Pro key only on this computer (not synced).
declarativeNetRequestLets Chrome itself apply the rules bundled in Vedello: send the Global Privacy Control signal and, with Pro, block trackers and strip tracking parameters from addresses. Vedello does not see the requests.
activeTabShows in the popup what Vedello did on the current tab and lets "Copy clean link" work on the tab where you click.
scriptingAdds the package's own scripts to pages to answer banners through the site's consent tool and send the GPC signal. No remote code.
alarmsEnds a timed pause and re-checks the Pro key about once a day.
contextMenusAdds "Copy clean link" to the right-click menu on a link (Pro).
privacy (optional)Asked only when you turn on a Chrome setting in Options: turn off third-party cookies, ad APIs and WebRTC leaks, and send Do Not Track (Pro).
cookies (optional)Asked only when you turn on "Clear a site's cookies when its last tab closes" (Pro). It only deletes cookies; it never reads them for anything else.
All sites (<all_urls>)Cookie banners appear on any site, so Vedello runs on every page to find the banner and press Reject. It only looks at the banner (and, with Pro, at newsletter boxes and at the address you copy). Nothing leaves your browser.

Network connections (version 1.0.0, tested 11 November 2026)

AddressWhen
api.polar.shOnly after you enter a Pro key: activation, then a check about once a day. No browsing data is sent.
buy.polar.shOnly when you tick the box and click Continue on the Pro screen: the checkout opens in a new tab.
pinchavelho.github.ioOnly if you click a link to our site.

We opened local test pages with cookie banners from several consent platforms, a newsletter pop-up, a paywall and links with tracking parameters, copied an address, opened every Vedello page, clicked Continue on the Pro screen and entered a test key, and recorded every request with the internet blocked. Nothing else was contacted. The tracker lists bundled in Vedello name many tracking services; those names are what Vedello blocks, not addresses it contacts.

Where your data is

On your computer only: settings, per-site exceptions, counts, history (Pro), trial date and Pro key in Chrome's extension storage; what was done on each open tab in memory until the tab closes; and the interface language you picked. Delete the history in Options, remove the key with "Remove key", or uninstall Vedello to delete everything.

Chrome Web Store

The privacy practices on the store listing are our own declaration, filled in to match this report. The link to the listing will be added here once the store approves Vedello.

Check it yourself

  1. Open chrome://extensions, find Vedello and click Details to see the exact permissions Chrome granted.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, click Inspect views: service worker, open the Network tab and use Vedello: only the addresses above can appear.
  3. For a full log, open chrome://net-export, record while you browse with Vedello and open the file in the NetLog viewer.
