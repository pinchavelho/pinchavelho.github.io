Vedello by Pinchavelho · extension for Chrome · in development

Say no to cookies, once.

Vedello answers cookie banners with “reject” on your behalf and tells websites you do not want to be tracked. It runs on your computer, collects nothing and sends nothing.

Chrome Web Store: coming soon

Vedello is still being built and tested. This page will grow before launch.

What it will do

Rejects cookie banners

Clicks “Reject all”, or unticks every purpose and saves, on the main consent platforms and on many sites with their own banner.

Sends privacy signals

Global Privacy Control tells every site you do not allow your data to be sold or shared.

You stay in charge

Pause it, or turn it off on a site that stops working. Statistics stay on your computer.

More with Pro

Blocks known third-party trackers in your own browser, objects to “legitimate interest”, cleans tracking codes from links and more.

What it won’t do

Paywalls and “consent or pay”

Vedello never hides or gets around paywalls, login or registration walls, paid content or “accept or pay” pages. Those are the site’s choice.

Not a VPN

No traffic goes through any server. Its blocking rules are our own and ship inside the extension; nothing is fetched from the internet while it runs.

Price

The essentials will be free. Vedello Pro will be sold on its own, with the same rules as Ritmello: pay what you think is fair, with a minimum of €1.99, VAT included, until 31 January 2027 (€2.99 from 1 February 2027); one-time payment; 15 days of Pro free from installation, no sign-up, no card.
